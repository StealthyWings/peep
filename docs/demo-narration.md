# Demo video narration (for ElevenLabs)

About 75 seconds read at a relaxed pace. Paste each section into ElevenLabs as its
own clip so you can line them up with the footage. Suggested shots are in italics.

---

**1. Hook**
*Shot: you outside, phone in hand, a bird calling off camera.*

You hear it before you see it. Something small, singing in a tree you walk past every day. What is it?

**2. Listen**
*Shot: tap Listen, hold the phone up, look at the tree, not the screen.*

Peep listens with BirdNET, an open model from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. It runs right here on the phone. No signal needed, nothing uploaded.

**3. The answer**
*Shot: the result card, then the cool fact.*

A common myna. And a fact you didn't know: it's on the list of the world's worst invasive species.

**4. Snap**
*Shot: snap a photo, tap the bird to zoom in.*

Prefer photos? Snap one. Google's open bird model picks it out, even when the bird is tiny in the frame.

**5. Stickers**
*Shot: sticker unlock animation, then the Life list shelf.*

Every new species earns you something. Not for tapping, for getting out. New places, early mornings, a whole week of fresh air.

**6. Flock**
*Shot: swipe through a friend's finds, the "all caught up" card, the weekly goal.*

Start a flock with family and friends. Swipe through what they found, then the feed ends, and tells you to go outside.

**7. Why open**
*Shot: airplane mode on, ID still works.*

Because every model is open, peep works in airplane mode, costs nothing to run, and your location never leaves your phone. Even many of the fun facts were picked out by Gemma, running on a laptop.

**8. Close**
*Shot: phone goes in the pocket, you look up.*

Peep. Spot birds, not screens.
